Design rules

Hierarchy
Score first, match state second, supporting statistics third.
Colour
VGK is always gold. The opponent remains blue regardless of home or away position.
Distance
The score and matchup must remain readable from across a room.
Motion
Only goal alerts animate. Normal updates use partial redraws to prevent flicker.
Resilience
Connection loss preserves the last score and adds a clear, quiet warning.
